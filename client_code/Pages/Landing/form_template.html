Questions

Is it free for students?

Yes. Health Booker never adds a booking fee. You only pay the studio for your pass.

How do payments work?

For now, you pay the studio directly at your first visit (card, cash or e-Transfer) and the front desk marks your pass as paid. Online card payments are coming.

What if I cancel late, or the studio cancels?

Each studio sets its own cancel window, and you see it before you book. If the studio cancels a class, your credit comes back automatically and you get a message.

Who sees my health notes?

Only the teachers of the studio you signed the waiver with, and only if you tick the box. Other studios see nothing.

What does it cost a studio?

Nothing while Health Booker is in early access. We will never take a commission on your bookings, and there is no contract.

Can my studio leave with its data?

Yes. Export clients, bookings and passes as CSV files any day, from Settings.

Is the app in French?

Classes are tagged by language (en français, bilingual) and the demo studios teach in both. The app itself is in English for now.
